Context & Motivation

The Problem with Conventional Attendance Systems

Traditional manual roll calls and RFID punch-cards introduce operational delays and severe fraud vulnerabilities.

Security Vulnerability

Proxy Marking & Buddy Punching

Paper register signatures are easily forged, and RFID cards are routinely tapped by friends for absent students.

Result: Zero authentic physical presence verification.
Classroom Inefficiency

10–15 Minutes Lost Per Lecture

Calling out 60+ student roll numbers consumes massive instructional time, resulting in over 40+ wasted lecture hours each semester.

Result: Significant instructional time wasted every week.
Administrative Overhead

Fragmented Records & Disputes

Scattered register books and Excel sheets prevent real-time visibility, sparking contentious eligibility disputes before exams.

Result: High administrative burden & audit vulnerability.

Core Biometric Engine

Deep Learning Biometric Pipeline

Precision sub-second biometric recognition executing from raw frame capture to database verification.

Stage 01

Frame Ingestion

Client captures high-definition RGB frame via HTML5 Canvas WebRTC, normalizing resolution and color channels.

Input Frame
Stage 02

YuNet Detection

Deep ONNX neural network identifies bounding box coordinates and extracts 5 facial landmarks (eyes, nose, mouth corners).

5 Landmarks
Stage 03

SFace Embedding

Face is aligned with affine transformation; SFace CNN extracts 128-dimensional L2-normalized float embeddings.

128-D Vector
Stage 04

Cosine Matching

High-speed cosine distance matched against enrolled student embeddings. Positive attendance if similarity > 0.58.

Verified Match

Biometric Security

Anti-Spoofing & Active Liveness Protection

Multi-tiered defensive algorithms thwart static photographs, smartphone screen replays, and video spoofs.

Passive Quality Verification

Autonomous Frame Analysis

Laplacian Variance Blur Detection

Rejects motion-blurred or low-quality camera frames to ensure only high-fidelity embeddings are registered.

Illumination & Glare Balance

Analyzes pixel intensity histograms to detect washouts, deep shadows, and backlight distortions.

Proximity & Scale Checking

Verifies that the face occupies optimal frame proportion without extreme digital zoom or truncation.

Active Challenge Verification

Interactive Motion Challenges

Randomized Action Prompts

Prompts user to perform real-time actions: Blink, Smile, Turn Head Left, or Turn Head Right before enrollment.

Replay Attack Prevention

Printed photographs and static screens fail randomized sequence timing and 3D angle verification.

Duplicate Face Alert

Compares new face embeddings against all enrolled students to prevent duplicate identity registration.
